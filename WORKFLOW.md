# CV Tailoring Workflow

Use three mandatory stages for every new job. Do not generate a tailored CV
before Stage 1 is approved.

## Stage 1: Research and fit brief

Inputs:

- complete job description;
- current CV and `my_background.md`;
- quick company research.

Research the company using its official website first, then recent credible
sources when needed. Identify:

- what the company and relevant business unit do;
- product, market, regulatory, technical, or organizational context;
- current challenges that plausibly affect this role.

Cite sources. Separate confirmed facts from interpretation. Never present an
inferred company challenge as confirmed.

Produce a brief that fits on one screen:

1. **Fit score: X/10**
   - responsibilities: 0-4;
   - domain: 0-2;
   - technical environment: 0-2;
   - seniority and hard constraints: 0-2.
2. **Positioning thesis** — one sentence explaining why Daniel is relevant.
3. **Highlight** — strongest evidence to foreground.
4. **Weak points** — real gaps, contradictions, and recruiter risks.
5. **Omit or de-emphasize** — information that weakens this application or
   consumes space without supporting the thesis.
6. **Open questions** — only facts whose answers could materially change the
   score or CV strategy.

Stop for user approval. Research and analysis may continue; CV editing may not.

## Stage 2: Tailoring plan and execution

After approval, translate the brief into exact changes.

1. Map important job requirements to direct, transferable, inferred, missing,
   or unsupported evidence.
2. Select keywords only when a bullet, project, skill, or confirmed experience
   proves them.
3. Choose one top-bar role and the most relevant defensible title lens for each
   broad startup role.
4. Decide experience and project selection, ordering, bullet emphasis, and
   omissions.
5. Rewrite summary, titles, bullets, projects, and skills.
6. Produce a reviewable diff with warnings for unresolved facts.
7. Generate tailored YAML and DOCX only after the proposal is approved.

Confidence is required. Fabricated technologies, responsibilities, dates,
credentials, authority, metrics, or outcomes are forbidden.

## Stage 3: Verification

Verify content:

- one clear hiring argument;
- concise language with no repetition or empty adjectives;
- important keywords supported by evidence;
- titles consistent with bullets;
- reverse chronology, except approved overlapping-role exceptions;
- all warnings and unresolved facts remain visible;
- no rule from `GUIDELINES.md` is violated.

Verify document:

- maximum two pages;
- correct experience order;
- consistent fonts, spacing, alignment, tabs, and links;
- valid DOCX archive with no unresolved placeholders;
- native PDF export matches DOCX;
- both PDF pages visually inspected.

Deliver:

- research and fit brief;
- review/diff;
- tailored YAML;
- DOCX;
- PDF;
- short list of unresolved factual questions, if any.
